# Chapter One Boss Portraits

Historical v2 record: the physician remains current; the traitor and performer have been superseded by the assets and animation described in `ARMED_BOSS_V3.md`.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. The accepted white/green physician is retained. The traitor and drummer were revised after feedback to emphasize visible pixel blocks and structural cyberpunk machinery, not generic historical fantasy.

## Final assets

- `boss_healer_pixel_v2.png`: white/green elderly physician, injector, vine armor and sprouting plant (accepted version).
- `boss_traitor_pixel_v2.png`: Lu Bu carrying Diao Chan, two helmet plumes, mechanical heavy armor, cyan circuitry and magenta accents.
- `crazy_performer_pixel_v2.png`: bare-chested adult drummer with covered waist, mechanical limbs, electronic drumsticks, giant amplified war drum and power cables.

All three originals load directly through `BossPortraitTextures` and render on transparent 256 × 192 logical canvases using nearest-neighbor sampling. No combat rules, stats, equipment or AI were changed. Older portraits remain on disk but are no longer referenced by these three battle models.
