# Forgotten Three Kingdoms — v0.4.0 Development Update

This update is a major combat, progression, UI, and presentation pass for **Forgotten Three Kingdoms**.

## Combat improvements

- Damage now resolves in the correct order: flat bonuses first, then multipliers.
- Attack attributes and damage-source information are clearer across combat and battle logs.
- Skill selections can now include character skills, with activation text and portrait feedback during battle.
- Peach and Wine rescue now wait for passive survival skills such as Unyielding, Nirvana, and Drumbeat to resolve first.
- Fixed multiple skill and status issues, including fixed-HP effects, Po Jun, Beng Huai, Charm shields, Time Hourglass, and Curse/Evil Aura behavior.
- Added equipment-trigger messages to battle logs and refreshed damage-preview typography.

## Bosses, enemies, and characters

- Strengthened the Chapter 2 boss roster and reduced the pressure of the Traitor boss fight.
- Updated Liu Bei, Guan Yu, and Zhang Fei AI behavior.
- Reworked Royal Death Guard, Giant Pus Sac, Zhen Ji, Pang Tong, Mi Heng, and several Chapter 2 boss interactions.
- Fixed Qixing Altar boss enhancement and Pang Tong's Nirvana label.
- Added portraits for every playable hero.

## Equipment, events, and progression

- Enhanced Thunder Spear and Fire-Pattern Silver Spear.
- Reworked Soul Stone, Red Hare, and Bloodsweat Horse.
- Added a 150-gold shop purchase for a choice of one of three chips.
- Added item-detail tooltips to shop price UI.
- Updated item pools, equipment placement, initial decks, the opening-fate pool, Unexpected Short Circuit, and several event rewards.
- Rebalanced Baigong Duoshang and Post-Battle Dividend.
- Changed the Chapter 2 completion chip reward to a Defense/HP chip.

## UI and visual work

- Rebuilt the main menu with dynamic presentation effects.
- Added Continue Game: return to the main menu during a run, then resume from the saved state.
- Reworked hero selection: select a hero first, then press **Awaken** to begin.
- Improved event HUD spacing, inventory/equipment UI, and hero-information hover behavior.
- Added Chapter 4 Abyss portraits for Scout, Giant Maw, Abyss Symbiote, and Phantom Tentacle in a Cthulhu cyberpunk pixel-art style.

## Known issue

Elite enemy portrait replacement is still in progress.

Thanks for following the development of **Forgotten Three Kingdoms**. Feedback is always welcome.
